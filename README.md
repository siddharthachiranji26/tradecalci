# Tradecalc

A browser-based trading position calculator for estimating fees, profit and loss, break-even price, and return on invested capital.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser. Enter your investment, leverage, entry and exit prices, fees, and trade direction. Save scenarios to keep them in this browser.

The calculator uses prices you enter; it does not provide live market data. Its estimates exclude funding, slippage, taxes, liquidation risk, and other exchange charges. Leveraged trading can result in losses beyond your investment.
